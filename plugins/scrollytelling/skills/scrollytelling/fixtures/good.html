Saturday market run: one oven vs two

One oven, twelve loaves

On a normal Saturday the single oven bakes twelve loaves and the queue is out the door by nine. Nothing is wrong; it is simply full.

The complication

A wedding order for forty loaves lands on the same morning. The oven cannot do both, and the market stall would go short.

What a second oven changes

With two ovens the wedding order and the market stall both ship. The cost is a second bake schedule and about nine hundred dollars of equipment.

The ask

Approve the second oven for the autumn season. If the wedding orders do not repeat, resell it at roughly two thirds of the price.
